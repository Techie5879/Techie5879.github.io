There is something about being in transit that makes the brain cooperate. No Wi-Fi on a 6 AM
train through the Konkan coast, nothing to do but stare at paddy fields and think. Half the ideas
I have come from those in-between hours when I am technically going somewhere but functionally
doing nothing.

## Why write about travel at all

Not because the destinations are remarkable. Most of them are not. But travel is the only time I
consistently slow down enough to notice things: the way light hits a building at 4 PM, how
different cities sound at night, what people eat for breakfast when nobody is performing culture
for tourists.

## A short list of things I remember

Not places. Moments.

- The mass of pigeons lifting off the steps of Jama Masjid at dusk, a sound like a curtain being shaken out
- Drinking absurdly sweet chai from a clay cup on a platform in Varanasi at 5 AM, cold enough to see your breath
- A completely silent courtyard inside the Alhambra where every surface was doing math
- The Tokyo subway at rush hour, where millions of people somehow never touch each other

### And so does regular prose

The real point of `.mdx` is not that you need components in every post. Most posts will be plain
prose and that is fine. The point is that when you want something richer -- a custom aside, an
interactive chart, a styled quote card, an embedded map -- you can reach for a component without
leaving the writing flow. You import it at the top of the file and use it like an HTML tag.

For posts that are just text, `.md` is simpler and there is no reason to change. Use `.mdx` when
plain markdown is not enough.
